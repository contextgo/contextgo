# Platform Specs

Reference table for default platform specs. Skills read this file before producing per-channel asset variants.

These values are defaults. The real platform spec must always be confirmed against the live channel before publish.

## Paid Networks

- Meta feed: 1:1, 4:5, 16:9 - headline up to 40 chars - body up to 125 chars - CTA from supported list
- Meta stories / reels: 9:16 - keep critical content inside the safe zone
- TikTok feed: 9:16 - copy under 100 chars in caption
- LinkedIn single image: 1.91:1 - body up to 150 chars for best surfacing
- X promoted post: 16:9 or 1:1 - body up to 280 chars including CTA
- YouTube in-stream: 16:9 with captions when sound-off

## Social Organic

- Instagram feed: 1:1 or 4:5 with carousel up to 10 cards
- Instagram stories / reels: 9:16
- TikTok organic: 9:16 with hook in first 2 seconds
- X organic: 16:9 or 1:1
- LinkedIn organic: 1.91:1, document carousels up to 20 pages
- YouTube shorts: 9:16

## Ecommerce Surfaces

- product hero KV: per-platform aspect, hero claim must survive at 320 wide
- listing card: typically 1:1, primary claim plus single proof point
- promotion banner: web hero 16:5, mobile 5:6 or 16:9, copy short
- selling-point images: vertical PDP modules, sized to platform module guidelines

## Email

- header banner: typically 600 wide max, retina-friendly artwork
- supporting blocks: kept under 600 wide for client compatibility

## In-Venue / Event

- digital signage: confirm venue spec before generation
- printed signage: confirm bleed and resolution before generation
