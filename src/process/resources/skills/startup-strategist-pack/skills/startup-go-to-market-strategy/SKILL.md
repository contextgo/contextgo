# Startup Go-To-Market Strategy

Use this skill when the startup needs the smallest credible route to customers.

## Use when

- The startup has a product concept and needs an initial GTM motion.
- The user wants a launch or early distribution plan.
- Messaging and channel choice must be tied to a specific segment.

## Do not use when

- The target segment is still undefined.
- The user expects a broad marketing calendar with no strategic focus.
- The work is purely product specification.

## GTM failures to avoid

- choosing too many channels at once
- writing abstract messaging disconnected from customer pain
- assuming awareness is the same as adoption
- planning launch theatrics without proof assets
- skipping the metrics that would show whether the motion works

## Workflow

### 1. Confirm the beachhead segment

Restate:

- who we are targeting first
- what trigger makes them pay attention

### 2. Define the conversion moment

Clarify:

- what action counts as meaningful traction
- what user behavior shows the startup is delivering value

### 3. Choose the primary motion

Select one dominant path first:

- founder-led sales
- product-led
- community-led
- partner-led
- content or inbound led

Secondary channels may exist, but only after the primary motion is clear.

### 4. Shape the messaging

Translate the value proposition into:

- core message
- proof points
- objections to address
- channel-specific adaptation

### 5. Define proof assets and feedback loops

Examples:

- demos
- case studies
- pilots
- testimonials
- landing pages
- onboarding proof

### 6. Build the 90-day motion

Return a focused plan with:

- first 30 days
- next 30 days
- following 30 days

### 7. Attach metrics

Track:

- acquisition and activation
- conversion to value
- early retention or repeat behavior
- channel quality

## Output format

Return:

### 1. Beachhead segment and trigger

### 2. Primary GTM motion

### 3. Core messaging and objections

### 4. Channels and why they fit

### 5. Proof assets to create

### 6. 90-day execution plan

### 7. Metrics and feedback loops

### 8. Major GTM risks
