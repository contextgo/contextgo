# Startup Startup Canvas

Use this skill when the startup needs a coherent strategy artifact, not scattered notes.

## What the canvas must do

The canvas must keep **strategy** and **business model** distinct but connected.

Strategy answers:

- who we serve
- what problem matters most
- why we can matter
- what tradeoffs define the venture

Business model answers:

- how value becomes revenue
- what cost structure the startup must support

## Use when

- The user needs a founder-level startup canvas.
- A startup idea must be turned into coherent strategic choices.
- Several moving parts exist, but they are not yet tied together.

## Do not use when

- The user only needs a GTM plan.
- The request is a narrow execution artifact such as a PRD or sprint plan.
- The business is mature enough that corporate planning tools are a better fit.

## Canvas failures to avoid

- mixing strategy and monetization into one vague paragraph
- claiming multiple primary segments
- listing activities instead of tradeoffs
- calling metrics "important" without selecting the few that matter
- treating defensibility as a slogan rather than a system of reinforcing choices

## Canvas structure

### 1. Vision

- what change the startup wants to create
- why the mission matters

### 2. Beachhead segment

- first segment to win
- why this segment comes first

### 3. Problem and value proposition

- what problem matters
- how the product changes the before/after state

### 4. Strategic posture

- low cost, differentiated value, or another deliberate posture
- what this implies for product and GTM choices

### 5. Tradeoffs

- what we will not do
- what opportunities we will consciously ignore for now

### 6. Growth motion

- the initial distribution or GTM path
- the channel or motion that deserves primary focus

### 7. Capabilities and constraints

- what must exist for the strategy to work
- what the team has versus still lacks

### 8. Defensibility

- what competitors cannot or will not copy
- how the choices reinforce one another

### 9. Cost structure and revenue streams

- how the startup spends money
- how it plans to make money
- biggest model uncertainties

### 10. Key metrics and must-be-true hypotheses

- North Star direction
- critical hypotheses that must be validated next

## Output format

Return:

### 1. Vision

### 2. Beachhead segment

### 3. Problem and value proposition

### 4. Strategic posture

### 5. Tradeoffs

### 6. Growth motion

### 7. Capabilities and constraints

### 8. Defensibility

### 9. Cost structure and revenue streams

### 10. Key metrics

### 11. Must-be-true hypotheses and next tests
