# Content And Writing Studio

ContextGo is not strongest when you ask AI to finish everything in one shot.

It is stronger when you use it as a writing system for:

- sources
- outlines
- drafts
- revision
- publishing preparation

## Good fit if...

- you write long-form content, docs, plans, or scripts
- you revise work repeatedly
- your source materials are spread across many places

## Starter Kit

- Recommended Workbench: `Conversation Cowork Workbench`
- Recommended Connectors: `Browser Context`, `Project Docs`, `Writing Sources`
- Recommended Runtime: `General writing runtime`
- Recommended Agent Package: `Writing / Research package`
- Recommended Skills: `web`, `markdown`, `docs`, `summary`, `citation`
